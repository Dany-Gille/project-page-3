At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, each child is cared through a comprehensive approach

Welcome to Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC). From early childhood to Senior High School, our co-educational campuses offer a global standard of learning that prepares students for the real world. By pairing time-tested Montessori values with modern teaching, we help every child build independence, confidence, and practical life skills. Discover our programs and see how our supportive community empowers young learners to lead and thrive in a changing future.
